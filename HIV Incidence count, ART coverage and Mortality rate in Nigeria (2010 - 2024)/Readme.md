# Title: HIV in Nigeria — Adult Incidence, ART Coverage and Mortality count (2010–2024)
File: HIV Incidence count, ART coverage and Mortality count in Nigeria (2010 - 2024) <br>
Main Analysis sheet: Pivot_Analysis

### Overview:
This project summarizes national trends in adult HIV incidence, Antiretroviral Treatment (ART) coverage, and AIDS-related mortality in Nigeria (2010–2024) using pivot analyses. <br> The sheet titled (Pivot_Analysis) contains the primary results: yearly incidence and mortality counts, ART coverage percentages, year-on-year changes, CAGR calculations, pre/post policy comparisons, milestone tracking, gap-to-target analysis, and a simple lagged regression linking ART coverage with next-year mortality.

### Key findings (high level):
• Incidence fell by 72% (110k → 31k). <br>
• ART coverage rose from 19% → 87%; gap to 95% target = 8 p.p. in 2024. <br>
• Mortality fell by 60% (75k → 30k). <br>
• Strong negative association observed between ART coverage and both incidence and next-year mortality.
